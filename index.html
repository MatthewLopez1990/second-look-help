Second Look help

Second Look is an iPhone app. It helps you check a text, an email, or a phone call that worries you.

It answers "No warning signs found," "Not sure," or "Likely a scam." It gives one reason and one thing to do.

If you already paid or shared private details, call your bank now. Use the number on your card. Do not use a number from the message. In an emergency, call 911.

How to check a text message

  1. Open Second Look. Tap "A text message."
  2. Tap "Open Messages" at the bottom. Find the message you are not sure about. If a new message opens, tap the X at the top.
  3. Take a picture of the screen: press the side button and the volume up button together, then let go.
  4. Come back: tap "Second Look" at the top left of the screen.
  5. The first time, Second Look asks "May I look at your newest screenshot?" Tap "Yes, look," or "No, I will pick it myself."
  6. On "Is this the one?", tap the messages to check. Then tap "Check this message."

Another way: in Messages, touch and hold the message, then tap Copy. Open Second Look. Tap "A text message," then "Other ways," then "I copied it." Tap Paste.

How to check an email

  1. Tap "An email." Pick where the email is: "The Mail app," another email app, or "I copied it."
  2. Take a picture of the email and come back. If the email is long, scroll down and take more pictures.

Or send the whole email. This also shows where its links really go. Tap "Send me the whole email" and follow the steps: open the email, tap Print, tap the Share button, then tap Second Look.

How to check a phone call

Tap "A phone call." Answer a few short questions about what the caller said. Second Look never listens to your calls.

If you already paid or shared private details

Call your bank now. Use the number on your card. Do not use a number from the message.

In Second Look, tap "I already paid or shared something" for the next steps.

The family circle: ask a family member

A family circle lets a family member give you a second opinion inside the app. Everyone needs an iPhone with Second Look, signed in to iCloud. It goes best when you sit together.

For the family member who helps

  1. Open Second Look. On the first screen, tap "I am helping a family member."
  2. Tap "Make my circle."
  3. Tap "Send the invite." Send it by text to the person you help. Tell them the link is real.
  4. To invite someone else later, go to your "Second looks" screen. Tap "Your family circle," then "Invite someone." A circle holds up to five people.

For the person who gets help

  1. Put Second Look on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the invite link your family member sent. Second Look opens.
  3. Tap "Join the circle."

How to ask

  1. Check a message. On the answer, tap the "Ask" button with your helper's name.
  2. Second Look shows who will see it. Tap the Send button.
  3. You can tap "Take it back" until your helper opens it.
  4. When your helper answers, a notification says "Your helper answered your second look." Tap it, or open Second Look, and tap "See the answer."

How the helper answers

  1. A notification says "Someone in your circle wants a second look." Tap it.
  2. Read the message and what Second Look found.
  3. If you like, type a short note first. Then tap "It's fine," "It's a scam," or "Call me first."

How to leave

  • Open Settings. Under "Family circle," tap "Leave this circle."
  • If you made the circle, tap "End this circle." It ends for everyone.
  • To delete a check you sent, tap More, then Past checks, then Delete under the check.

Someone you trust

This works without a family circle. The other person does not need the app.

  1. Open Settings. Tap "Add someone you trust."
  2. Tap "Choose from my contacts," or type a name and a phone number.
  3. After a check, tap the "Ask" button with their name. Second Look opens a text for you. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.

Faster ways to open Second Look

These are extras. You never need them.

A button on your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty space on your Home Screen.
  2. Tap "Edit" at the top, then tap "Add Widget."
  3. Type Second Look in the search box, then tap it.
  4. Pick a size, then tap "Add Widget." Tap "Done."

A button on your Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen. Tap "Customize," then tap the Lock Screen picture.
  2. Tap the space under the clock.
  3. Find Second Look and tap "Check a message." Tap "Done."

A button in Control Center

  1. Swipe down from the top right corner of the screen.
  2. Touch and hold an empty space. Tap "Add a Control."
  3. Type Second Look in the search box. Tap "Check a message."

Practice

Tap More, then "Try a practice check." It uses a pretend message. Nothing is sent.

Please note

  • Second Look gives guidance. It can be wrong.
  • "No warning signs found" does not mean a message is safe.
  • It does not block messages or calls.
  • It does not replace your bank or the police.

Accessibility

Second Look is built to work with VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, larger text, and reduced motion. Every result can be read aloud. If something is hard to use, please tell us.

Buying the app

Second Look is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There is no subscription. For a refund, use Apple's refund process at reportaproblem.apple.com.
