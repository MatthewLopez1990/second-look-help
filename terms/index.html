Second Look Terms of Use

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Second Look is an iPhone app made by Matthew Lopez, an individual app developer in the United States. In the App Store, the app is called "Second Look: Family Scam Check."

Your licence to use the app

Your right to use Second Look is given by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

We do not add a licence of our own. This page explains, in plain words, what the app can and cannot do.

What Second Look can and cannot do

  • Second Look gives guidance. It can be wrong. A scam can look normal, and a real message can look like a scam.
  • "No warning signs found" does not mean a message is safe. It means Second Look did not find the signs it looks for.
  • Second Look does not block messages or calls. It cannot prove who really sent a message.
  • Second Look does not replace your bank or the police. It does not give legal, money, or medical advice.
  • If you think you lost money or shared private details, call your bank now. Use the number on your card. Do not use a number from the message.
  • In an emergency, call 911.
  • You decide what to do about any message. Answers from your family, from someone you trust, or from another app can also be wrong.
  • Second Look may show links and phone numbers for official places to report a scam. Others run those places.

Who it is for

Second Look is for adults in the United States. It is in English.

Buying the app

Second Look is a one-time purchase on the App Store. There is no subscription. Apple handles the payment. To ask for a refund, use Apple's own refund process at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The family circle

  • The family circle uses your iCloud account. Everyone in it needs an iPhone with Second Look, signed in to iCloud.
  • What you send to your circle is kept in the iCloud space of the person who made the circle. Apple's iCloud terms apply to it.
  • Only invite people you trust. Each invitation link lets one person join. Do not post it where others can see it.
  • You can leave a circle at any time: open Settings and tap "Leave this circle." If you made the circle, you can end it for everyone: tap "End this circle."
  • You can delete a check you sent, from your phone and from your helper's phone.
